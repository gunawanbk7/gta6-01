SOLMAR BAY

AN OPEN-WORLD CRIME STORY

Mara Voss is back in Solmar Bay — a sun-soaked coastal city of palms, piers and fast money — with $120, a borrowed name and nothing to lose. Rico Vane, the fixer of the marina, has four jobs that pay. The Solmar Bay Police Department has other ideas. Steal cars, outrun the law, finish the jobs, and take the city.

E steal / enter car  ·  Space handbrake  ·  F punch  ·  2 pistol, Click / J shoot, R reload
Esc / P pause & save  ·  M mute  ·  Progress saves automatically after every mission.
